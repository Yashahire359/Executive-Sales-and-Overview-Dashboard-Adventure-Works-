# 📊 Executive Sales and Overview Dashboard | Adventure Works

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Executive Sales and Overview Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI and the Adventure Works dataset.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, revenue, profit, product trends, and regional performance. It helps business stakeholders monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and business performance.
- Monitor key sales and profit indicators.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Evaluate regional sales performance.
- Understand sales trends over time.
- Present business insights through interactive visualizations.
- Support executive-level reporting and decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Sales Overview
- Overall sales performance monitoring.
- Key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Sales and profit overview.
- Interactive filters for exploring business performance.

### 2. Sales Performance Analysis
- Analysis of sales trends over time.
- Comparison of sales performance across different periods.
- Identification of business growth patterns.

### 3. Product Analysis
- Identification of top-performing products.
- Product category performance analysis.
- Comparison of product sales and profitability.

### 4. Regional Sales Analysis
- Analysis of sales across different regions.
- Comparison of regional business performance.
- Identification of high-performing markets.

### 5. Interactive Reporting
- Dynamic filtering and cross-filtering.
- Interactive charts and tables.
- Drill-down analysis, where configured.
- Easy exploration of sales and profitability metrics.
